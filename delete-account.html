Delete Your Account
You can permanently delete your dartova account and all associated data directly from within the app. Follow the steps below.
1
Open dartova on your device Make sure you're logged in to the account you want to delete
2
Tap the Settings icon Located in the bottom navigation bar or top-right corner of the home screen
3
Scroll down and tap "Delete Account" Found in the Account section at the bottom of Settings
4
Confirm the deletion Read the warning, type the confirmation phrase, and tap Delete
⚠ This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
Deleting your account will immediately remove your profile, match history, training statistics, and all personal data from our servers. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately via Google Play or the App Store.
