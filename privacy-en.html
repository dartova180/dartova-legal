Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 11, 2025

1. Introduction

dartova ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Display name (chosen by you)
  • Firebase User ID (automatically generated unique identifier)
  • Authentication provider (Google, Apple, or Email)

2.2 Game Data

We collect and store:

  • Game results and scores
  • Training session data
  • Personal records and statistics
  • Game preferences and settings
  • Date and time of games played

2.3 Subscription Data (PRO Users)

For users with PRO subscriptions:

  • Subscription status (active/inactive)
  • Plan type (monthly/yearly)
  • Subscription start and expiration dates
  • Purchase history (managed by RevenueCat and Google Play/App Store)

2.4 Technical Data

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash reports and error logs (if app crashes)

2.5 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect:

  • Your precise location
  • Photos or camera access
  • Contacts or phone information
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Payment card details (handled by Google Play/App Store)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Service: Enable game functionality, save progress, and sync data
  • Authentication: Verify your identity and manage your account
  • Subscription Management: Process payments and manage PRO features
  • Improve the App: Analyze usage patterns and fix bugs
  • Customer Support: Respond to your inquiries and provide assistance
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share limited data with the following third-party services:

4.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Purpose: Authentication, database, and app analytics
  • Data shared: Email, user ID, game data
  • Privacy Policy: Firebase Privacy

4.2 RevenueCat

  • Purpose: Subscription management and payment validation
  • Data shared: User ID, subscription status, purchase data
  • Privacy Policy: RevenueCat Privacy

4.3 Google Play Store / Apple App Store

  • Purpose: Payment processing and subscription management (required)
  • Data shared: Purchase information, subscription status
  • Privacy: Managed directly by Google/Apple

Important: We never sell your personal data to third parties. Data is only shared with essential service providers to make the app function.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: dartova180@gmail.com

6. Data Storage and Security

6.1 Where We Store Data

  • Firebase Firestore: User profiles, game data, statistics
  • Device Storage: Temporary cache and local preferences
  • RevenueCat: Subscription information

6.2 How We Protect Data

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase security rules to prevent unauthorized access
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security updates

6.3 Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data stored indefinitely while account is active
  • After account deletion: Data permanently removed within 30 days
  • Subscription data: Retained as required by tax law (typically 5-7 years)
  • Crash logs: Deleted after 90 days

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence, including the United States (where Firebase and RevenueCat servers are located). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by GDPR.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The App uses minimal tracking:

  • Authentication tokens: To keep you logged in
  • Local preferences: To remember your settings
  • No advertising cookies: We don't track you for ads

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notification
  • Email notification
  • Updated date at the top of this policy

Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Managing Your Subscription

To cancel your PRO subscription:

  • Android: Manage Subscriptions
  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → dartova
  • In-App: Settings → Manage Subscription
